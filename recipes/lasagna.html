Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian oven-baked pasta dish made by layering wide, flat pasta sheets with savory sauces, meat or vegetables, and cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 diced onion, 2 minced garlic cloves
  • 1 can (15-28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1-2 cans tomato sauce
  • 1-2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1-2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano

Steps

  1. Cook 1 pound of ground beef or a mix of beef and Italian sausage in a large skillet over medium heat with a diced onion and minced garlic until browned. Drain the excess fat.
  2. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce or paste, and seasonings (basil, oregano, salt, pepper, and a pinch of sugar). Simmer for 15 to 30 minutes until thick.
  3. In a medium bowl, combine ricotta or cottage cheese, grated Parmesan, an egg, and a portion of the shredded mozzarella. Season with a little salt and pepper.
  4. Prep the pan: Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish so the noodles do not slide.
  5. First layer: Place a single layer of lasagna noodles (cooked al dente or oven-ready) across the bottom.
  6. Add fillings: Spread a portion of the cheese mixture over the noodles, then top with a layer of meat sauce and a sprinkle of mozzarella.
  7. Repeat: Repeat the layers (noodles, cheese mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella) 2 to 3 times until you reach the top of the dish.
  8. Finish the top: End with a final layer of noodles, a thin layer of sauce, and a generous topping of mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  9. Bake: Cover the dish tightly with aluminum foil and bake at 375°F for about 25 to 40 minutes (remove the foil for the last 10 minutes if you want a browned, bubbly top).
  10. Let the lasagna rest for 10 to 15 minutes before slicing and serving so the layers hold their shape.
