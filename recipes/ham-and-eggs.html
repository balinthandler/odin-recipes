Ham and Eggs

Description

Ham and eggs is a classic hot dish made from ham and eggs, usually enjoyed for breakfast, lunch, or a quick dinner.

Ingredients

  • 2 large fresh eggs (per serving)
  • 2 slices of ham (or a ham steak)
  • 1.5 teaspoons of olive oil or butter (unsalted or salted)
  • A pinch of salt and black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Toss in ham and cook for 1 to 2 minutes.
  2. Flip ham and carefully crack an egg on top of each slice. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and cook until ham is caramelized, egg whites are just set, and yolks are soft, 2 1/2 to 3 minutes.
